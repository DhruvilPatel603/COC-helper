Privacy Policy

Your privacy and security are paramount. This policy outlines how the Clash of Clans Discord Bot collects, processes, and protects your data.

Last Updated: August 29, 2026 • Effective Immediately

Minimal Storage

We only store publicly available Clash stats and necessary Discord server identifiers.

Zero Personal Data

We do not read, store, or log private messages, voice data, emails, or personal identities.

User Control

Server owners and members can unlink accounts or wipe guild data at any time.

1

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Clash of Clans Discord Bot (referred to as "the Bot", "Service", "we", or "us"). By inviting, configuring, or interacting with the Bot in your Discord server, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

The Bot is an automated tool engineered to track Clash of Clans clan wars, Clan War Leagues (CWL), Raid Weekends, and provide real-time alerts and analytics via Discord.

2

Information We Collect

To provide core services, the Bot collects and stores the following strictly required categories of data:

Discord Identifiers

Guild IDs: To identify connected Discord servers and persist server-specific preferences.
Channel IDs: To deliver attack feeds, automated reminders, and live war status boards to your configured channels.
User IDs: Collected only when a member voluntarily links their Clash player tag via /player link or executes administrative commands.

Public Clash of Clans Game Data

Retrieved directly from the official Supercell Clash of Clans REST API: Clan tags, clan names, war state (stars, destruction %, attack order), player tags, in-game names, Town Hall levels, hero levels, and Clan Capital raid records.

3

Information We DO NOT Collect

We respect your privacy. The Bot strictly does NOT collect or process:

  • Discord chat message contents or private direct messages (DMs).
  • Voice channel audio, microphone activity, or video streams.
  • User email addresses, real names, physical locations, or payment details.
  • Supercell ID credentials, account passwords, or API tokens.
4

How We Use Information

Stored data is utilized solely for legitimate operational features:

  • Diffing real-time clan war attacks and broadcasting live alert embeds.
  • Pinging verified clan members with pending attacks at designated reminder intervals (6h, 3h, 1h, 10m).
  • Maintaining live auto-updating war status dashboards in designated channels.
  • Computing historical clan analytics, win streaks, and 3-star performance metrics.
5

Storage, Retention & Security

Infrastructure: Data is persisted in an encrypted MySQL database hosted on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure with restricted firewall access and SSL-encrypted transport.

Retention Period: Configuration data is maintained as long as the Bot remains in your Discord guild. Historical war logs and attack events are retained to provide war history reports and prevent duplicate Discord alerts.

6

Data Deletion & User Rights

You have full ownership and control over your data:

  • Unlinking Player Tags: Any user can remove their linked player tag by running /player unlink.
  • Server Data Purge: Server administrators can reset all clan links and notification configurations using /admin reset_guild.
  • Bot Removal: Kicking or banning the Bot from your Discord server halts all polling and background monitoring for that guild immediately.
